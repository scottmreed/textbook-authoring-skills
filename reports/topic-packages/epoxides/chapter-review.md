##### `instr-001` — blocker · chemical-accuracy · confidence 0.95

- **Location** — `question_slug`: epox-two-routes-matrix-v2; `concept_slug`: epoxide-synthesis; `anchor_text`: Relative geometry of the substituents is carried into the product
- **Observation** — The answer key marks the feature 'Relative geometry of the substituents is carried into the product' as 'no' (does not apply) for the halohydrin-plus-base route. That is chemically wrong. Halohydrin ring closure is an intramolecular Williamson SN2 that proceeds with inversion at the carbon bearing the halide, so it is fully stereospecific: the relative configuration of the halohydrin determines the cis/trans configuration of the epoxide. The two diastereomeric 3-bromobutan-2-ols are distinct molecules (InChIKeys JCYSVJNMXBWPHS-DMTCNVIQSA-N and JCYSVJNMXBWPHS-IMJSIDKUSA-N) and, because the closure has a single fixed stereochemical course, they must close to the two distinct epoxide diastereomers (PQXKWPLDPFFDJP-ZXZARUISSA-N, the meso cis, and PQXKWPLDPFFDJP-QWWZWVQMSA-N, the chiral trans). The classic classroom demonstration of the same point is that trans-2-chlorocyclohexanol cyclizes to cyclohexene oxide while the cis diastereomer cannot, because the alkoxide and the C-Cl bond cannot reach an anti arrangement.
- **Learner impact** — A student who correctly knows that halohydrin closure is stereospecific is marked wrong, and the generic feedback then teaches the false generalization back to them ('there is no alkene geometry for it to preserve'). It builds the misconception that only concerted reactions transmit stereochemical information, which has to be un-taught before Williamson synthesis, SN2, and any anti-periplanar requirement is used again. It also puts this package in direct conflict with live content students may read the same week.
- **Evidence** — topic.package.json epox-two-routes-matrix-v2 answer_key [redacted]; feedback_bundle.generic_incorrect_explanation: 'The halohydrin route begins at a saturated compound ... there is no alkene geometry for it to preserve.' The live chapter-18 package (ethers-epoxides-thiols-and-sulfides, nugget-epoxide-preparation) states the opposite: 'This closure is nothing more than an intramolecular Williamson ether synthesis, and its SN2 character imposes the same anti requirement: the C-O bond forms anti to the departing C-Br bond.'
- **Recommended outcome** — The question must stop asserting that halohydrin cyclization discards substituent geometry. Either the feature has to be reworded so it unambiguously asks about ALKENE geometry (which the halohydrin route has none of), or the cell must be corrected to 'applies' with feedback that names the inversion at the C-X carbon. Whichever is chosen, the chapter needs to agree with the live chapter-18 statement that the closure is a stereospecific intramolecular Williamson displacement.
