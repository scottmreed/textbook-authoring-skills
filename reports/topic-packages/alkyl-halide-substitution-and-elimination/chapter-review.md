# Chapter review — Reactions of Alkyl Halides: Nucleophilic Substitution and Elimination (`alkyl-halide-substitution-and-elimination`)

_Reviewed 2026-07-24 · chapter version 1 · personas: Organic Chemistry Instructor, Struggling Student, Accessibility, Learner with Visual Preference · produced by the `review-organic-chapter` skill (first / baseline run)_

**Publication readiness: blocked**

A chemically strong, well-sequenced chapter with an excellent 30-item question bank — but it is not publishable as-is for two independent reasons, each confirmed against the source files. (1) It actively teaches **incorrect chemistry**: the flagship SN1 "racemization" video is scripted on tert-butyl chloride, an achiral substrate that cannot racemize. (2) Four graded question families' **accessibility descriptions hand assistive-technology users the answer** a sighted student must reason out, and the E2 hotspot has no non-visual way to select an atom other than that leaked answer — so a required activity is not completable for some learners except by being told the answer. The remaining findings (a promised-but-undrawn decision grid, stereochemistry and anti-periplanar geometry shown only in prose, an energy profile that lives only in assessment and has two equal-height transition states, dead "Background reading" links) are high/medium improvements. Fix the two blocker classes and this chapter moves to "ready with minor revisions."

## Top blockers
- **[BLOCKER · wrong chemistry] SN1 racemization taught on an achiral substrate** — the `video-sn1-two-step-racemization` storyboard rotates the tert-butyl cation and scripts "two mirror-image capture events … the racemic alcohol product," but the product `2-methyl-2-propanol (CC(C)(C)O)` is achiral and the cation's faces are homotopic — no enantiomers, no racemate. (Instructor `instr-001`, `nugget-sn1-mechanism`)
- **[BLOCKER · answer leak] Four question types state the graded answer in their accessibility description** — `curved_arrow` (`ch11-sn2-arrow`: "source is the hydroxide oxygen lone pair, destination is the carbon bearing bromine"), `hotspot` (`ch11-e2-zaitsev-hotspot`: "Click the internal beta carbon…"), `structure_scaffold` (`ch11-draw-sn2-alcohol`: "(expected: 1-propanol)"), and `synthesis_route` (`ch11-halide-to-amine`: names "azide"). A screen-reader user is handed what a sighted student must derive. (Accessibility `access-001/002/003/004`)
- **[BLOCKER · required activity not completable] E2 hotspot has no non-visual atom picker** — `ch11-e2-zaitsev-hotspot` ships only `molecule_smiles`+`select_count` with opaque `atom_1/atom_4` ids and no labeled selectable targets, so a non-pointer / screen-reader user cannot select the carbon except via the leaked description above. (Accessibility `access-005`; contingent — see disagreements)

## Top 5 recommended changes
1. **Re-script the SN1 racemization video on a chiral substrate** — need: a single-enantiomer substrate with a stereocenter at the reacting carbon so opposite-face capture genuinely gives an enantiomeric pair → **fix existing media** (figure/media, blocker). The achiral tert-butyl system may still illustrate ionization/kinetics.
2. **Strip the leaked answers from four question `accessible_description`s** — need: the non-visual text must convey the stimulus + task, not the verdict → **text-equivalent prose edit** (assessment, blocker). Lowest-effort, highest-value; mirrors the existing `find_accessibility_leaks` rule for the semantic leaks the automated guard can't catch.
3. **Give the E2 hotspot a keyboard-operable labeled atom target** (or an equivalent non-pointer item) — need: select the beta carbon without a mouse or sight → **interactive fallback** (interactive, blocker; verify renderer first).
4. **Draw the "decision grid" the prediction section names** — need: an at-a-glance substrate × reagent × solvent/temperature → pathway map → **static decision table** (figure, high; the `comparison_matrix` format already exists in the package, so no new capability).
5. **Put the SN1 energy profile in instruction, make it chiral-concrete, and fix the equal-height transition states** — need: the concerted-vs-stepwise contrast is assessed via an energy diagram (`ch11-sn1-energy-profile`) but never taught, its two maxima are drawn at equal `y=2.8` (contradicting its "highest TS" hint), and SN1 racemization is asserted but never shown on a stereocenter → **add a teaching figure + redraw + accessible data readout** (figure/assessment, high).

### Places where a description is sufficient (no new asset)
- The **static molecule figures'** existing `alt_text` is chemically substantive (names + the load-bearing feature, incl. R/S and the inversion relationship) — keep as-is; do **not** replace with heavier assets.
- The **text/select/rank/matrix/categorize/matching** question descriptions are already task-focused and non-leaking — no change.
- The mechanism **prose** is a genuine text equivalent for the (currently hidden) videos — the gap is the missing *visual/stepwise* form for sighted and struggling learners, not the non-visual equivalent.

### Retained disagreements (with resolution)
- **Energy profile — data readout vs visible diagram.** Accessibility (`access-006`) asks for a structured readout of the profile's control points; Visual Preference (`visual-007`) asks for a *visible* energy diagram in instruction. **Resolution:** not a conflict — rec-005 does both: add the diagram to the teaching section AND expose its control points as an accessible data table. One asset satisfies both perspectives.
- **Mechanism motion — animation vs text/static.** Struggling Student and Visual Preference want to *see* the transformation; Accessibility wants a non-visual equivalent (and warns a produced video needs captions/AD). **Resolution:** the least-complex option is a **static, labeled, arrow-pushing step sequence** (rec-006) plus the existing prose — this serves sighted, struggling, and AT learners without committing to producing (and captioning) an animation. Producing the videos is optional polish, not the required fix.
- **Hotspot completability (`access-005`) is contingent.** Both Accessibility and Instructor flagged that `atom_1/atom_4` map to rendered atom indices that can't be verified from the package. **Resolution:** treated as a blocker pending renderer verification — if the hotspot renderer already exposes a keyboard-operable labeled atom picker, `access-005` downgrades to the answer-leak of `access-002` and readiness rises to `major revision`.
